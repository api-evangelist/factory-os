# Factory OS

> Factory OS (styled Factory_OS) is an off-site construction company founded in 2017 in Vallejo, California that manufactures wood-frame multifamily and affordable housing modules on an assembly line and assembles them on site. It was recapitalized and renamed **Harbinger Homes** (Harbinger Production Inc.) in May 2024, and factoryos.com now 301-redirects every path to harbinger.homes. ## What the company builds

- Industrialized off-site manufacturing of wood-frame housing modules for multifamily and affordable housing, produced by union carpenters across roughly two dozen assembly stations in a 33-step process inside a 275,000 sq ft facility in Building 680 on Mare Island, Vallejo, California.
- Published claims: about 50% less production time and 25% or more lower production cost versus conventional site-built construction; more than 4,000 homes delivered across California and Hawaii, roughly 90% of them affordable housing.

## Corporate timeline

- 2017: Founded by Rick Holliday and Larry Pace; production begins at Mare Island in early 2018.
- 2021: Raises $60M from JPMorgan Chase and Saint-Gobain; roughly $132M raised across three rounds in total.
- May 2024: Recapitalized and renamed Harbinger Homes (Harbinger Production Inc.); Tom Smith becomes CEO and Kevin Brown president and chief commercial officer in July 2024.
- 12 February 2026: WARN notice filed covering all 280 employees, citing a lack of new business and loss of capital funding, with layoffs beginning 13 April 2026. The company stated it was not planning to shut its doors and was pursuing new contracts.
